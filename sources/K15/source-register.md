## Section 41 scope note (official law text cross-check)

The official Ministry of Commerce English text states that Copyright Law No.15/2019 section 41 limits only the related-rights protections in sections 37, 38, 39, 40 and 42 for the listed acts. It does not itself create an exception to the author's economic rights in section 18; those limitations are in Chapter XII, sections 24–33. Section 41(c) has an express face-to-face teaching carve-out for performances and phonograms published as teaching and teaching materials, and the section carries the normal-exploitation / legitimate-interests proviso. This is a textual scope note, not legal advice or a determination of any specific use. Official text: https://commerce.gov.mm/sites/default/files/documents/2024/05/Copyright%20Law%20%28English%29.pdf#page=29
